Sentient.

2022. Brand Identity & UI/UX.

BRIEFING

Sentient is a mobile application designed to educate users on gender issues through an intuitive interface and video call features. The project was developed as a university project abroad at Escola Superior de Artes e Design.

CONCEPT

The design emphasizes empathy through a gender neutral aesthetic and bold typography. The application creates a safe space for learning and discussion, allowing users to connect with educators and experts on gender-related topics through seamless video communication.
